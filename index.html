WeedDAO Interoperability Audit

Find the structural problems that make lab data difficult to reuse.

The audit examines supported laboratory exports for analyte naming, units, result and status semantics, metadata completeness, ambiguity, and schema-level issues that complicate exchange and downstream analysis.

01

Preserve the source

Original labels, values, and provenance remain intact. Normalized fields are added as a separate analytical layer rather than replacing source-native data.

02

Surface interoperability issues

Flag unmapped or ambiguous analytes, unit and status parsing issues, missing metadata, and structural exceptions that may require review.

03

Deliver usable outputs

Receive an executive summary, technical appendix, machine-readable JSON, and exception output designed for QA, data, and laboratory-systems teams.

What the audit reports

  • Analyte mapping coverage
  • Unit parse coverage
  • Status parse coverage
  • Metadata field presence
  • Ambiguous and unmapped labels
  • Human-review items
  • Structural exceptions
  • Recommended export-structure improvements

Current scope

Designed for supported CSV and JSON exports, including ordinary long-format data, Massachusetts-style composite labels, and compatible wide-format files.

PDF/image ingestion, direct LIMS access, and API integrations are not part of the current product scope.

Scope note

This audit evaluates data structure and interoperability characteristics of the supplied export. It does not determine laboratory analytical accuracy, regulatory compliance, accreditation status, or product safety.

Public-data research

Research informs the product.

Our research program studies how public cannabis laboratory records differ across reporting systems and how those differences can be handled without silently changing the meaning of source data.

01

Preserve source-native data

Original labels and values remain intact. Normalized values are treated as a separate analytical layer rather than replacements for source data.

02

Audit normalization

Analyte mappings, unit-like tokens, and schema assumptions are reviewed for collisions, ambiguity, and unsupported interpretation.

03

Validate across formats

We use public laboratory and state testing datasets to test schema adapters, provenance handling, deterministic behavior, and terminology coverage.

Principles

Useful infrastructure requires restraint.

WeedDAO is designed around transparent assumptions, clear limits, and preserving the distinction between what data structure can establish and what it cannot.

What we do

Study interoperability, document normalization assumptions, preserve source-native records, flag ambiguity, and use human review where automated interpretation should stop.

What we don't claim

No laboratory rankings, no blanket claims about lab accuracy, no consumer-health conclusions, no compliance certification, and no endorsement by cited data providers or agencies.
