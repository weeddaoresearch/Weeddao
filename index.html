Current research

Research readiness across public cannabis lab data

Our first pilot asks a practical question: how difficult is it to combine public cannabis laboratory records across different reporting systems without losing the meaning of the original data?

01

Preserve source-native data

Original labels and values remain intact. Normalized values are treated as a separate analytical layer rather than replacements for source data.

02

Audit normalization

Analyte mappings and unit-like tokens are reviewed for collisions, ambiguity, and assumptions before any external research claims are made.

03

Invite independent review

We are seeking methodological feedback from analytical chemistry and measurement-science experts before publication or expansion.

Principles

Useful research requires restraint.

WeedDAO is designed around transparent assumptions, clear limits, and preserving the distinction between what a dataset shows and what it cannot establish.

What we do

Study data interoperability, document normalization assumptions, preserve source-native records, and seek expert methodological review.

What we don't claim

No laboratory rankings, no blanket claims about lab accuracy, no consumer-health conclusions, and no endorsement by cited data providers or agencies.
